About us

Small by design, ambitious by nature.

Lanternfield began as two editors and a borrowed office. Today we publish around twenty titles a year, and we still read every submission ourselves.

We believe in slow publishing: long editorial relationships, fair royalties, and backlist books kept in print for as long as readers want them.

For writers

Our doors are open.

We accept unagented submissions twice a year, in March and September. We're especially keen on debut authors and underrepresented voices.
