Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

What this site is

This site hosts free browser games and tools. They run entirely in your web browser.

What we collect

We do not collect, sell or share personal information, and the games do not use advertising or tracking.

Data saved on your device

Some games save progress, settings or high scores in your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Optional online features

A few games may offer optional features such as accounts, cloud saves or leaderboards. If you choose to use them, the details you enter (for example a username) are sent to the service that stores them, only to provide that feature. Do not enter anything you do not want stored.

Discord

If a game is used inside Discord, Discord handles your sign-in and profile information under its own privacy policy. We only receive what Discord shares with the app, such as your display name.

Children

These games are meant for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
